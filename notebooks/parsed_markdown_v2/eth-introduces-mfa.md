## How to register for MFA at ETH

IT security is a subject that affects all companies and institutions. It is also an issue to which ETH pays considerable attention – hence the decision to introduce multi-factor authentication (MFA). Entering a second login confirmation is intended to increase the security of our user accounts and data and reduce the risk of cyberattacks. You can read more on the background to this decision in the Internal news article “ MFA: More security for your data” .
