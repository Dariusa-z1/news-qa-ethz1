## Main article

Since April 2019, a whole series of new functionalities have been available to the academic community of the platform e-rara.ch .

e-rara.ch now supports the IIIF standard, too. The corresponding IIIF manifest can be found for each of the 71,750 titles (as of 1 April 2019) on the platform. This means that texts, prints or maps can be compared with digital copies from numerous prestigious institutions in the IIIF-Community and researched in more detail on the screen.
