## Main article

Arik Jung is the new president of the Academic Association of Scientific Staff at ETH Zurich (AVETH). AVETH members convened on the evening of Wednesday, 2 March 2016, at Hönggerberg, where they voted on the successor to Florian Thöle.

Arik Jung is a doctoral student in the Laboratory for Solid State Physics (D-PHYS) and is the former treasurer of AVETH. In front of AVETH members, he expressed his intention to continue the good work done in recent years and maintain the excellent relationship with specialist associations and other university groups. He also commented that AVETH can make ETH an even better workplace.

His predecessor, Florian Thöle, was thanked for his hard work and dedication. He will now focus on his doctoral thesis in the Department of Materials (D-MATL).
