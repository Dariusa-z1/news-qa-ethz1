## Main article

When entering a reference in ETH E-Citations, select the option "Publish in E-Collection", which enables you to upload a full text directly for publication in ETH E-Collection . The ETH-Bibliothek team will then verify the legal parameters and publish your document. It also guarantees the link from ETH E-Citations .
