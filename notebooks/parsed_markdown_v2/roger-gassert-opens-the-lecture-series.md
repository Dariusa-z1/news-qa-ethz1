## Main article

In the RESC online lecture series, speakers from the network of the ETH competence centre Rehabilitation Engineering and Science (RESC) provide insights into key topics in rehabilitation - from care to research to personal experiences. In the first edition, Roger Gassert, Professor of Rehabilitation Engineering and Head of RELab at ETH Zurich, will share his own personal journey as a patient and identify major challenges in rehabilitation as well as milestones along his path to recovery. Please save the date of the lecture: You are cordially invited. 28 November 2022, 12.00 - 13.00. Closer to the date, the zoom link to the lecture will be published here .
