## Main article

CSNOW, the Network of Women in Computer Science (formerly Forum for Women in Computer Science), invites all interested students, faculty members and staff to join us as we take a step forward towards diversifying our environment.

Our mission is to support women before, during and after their Computer Science studies at ETH. To achieve this, we conduct a wide spectrum of activities, from introducing high school students to computer science, up to career coaching and preparing students for their first professional roles.

On Wednesday, 2nd of November, 5pm at CAB G51, we are relaunching our organization under a new name and logo. Event highlights include a message by the Head of Department, Prof. Dr. Emo Welzl and an address by the rector of ETH Zurich, Prof. Dr. Sarah M. Springman, followed by an apero riche.

More information here: www.frauen.inf.ethz.ch/archiv/2016/csnowlaunch .
