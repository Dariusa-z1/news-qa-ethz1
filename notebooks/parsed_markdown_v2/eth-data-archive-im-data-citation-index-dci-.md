## Main article

Moreover, the ETH Data Archive's metadata is integrated in the Web of Science Data Citation Index (DCI). The DCI receives notification following the registration of a DOI (Digital Object Identifier) for research data.

The Digital Curation office advises members of ETH Zurich on all aspects of research data management.
