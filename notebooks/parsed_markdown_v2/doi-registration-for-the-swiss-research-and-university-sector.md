## Main article

ETH Zurich's DOI Desk registers DOI names for scientific literature and research data in Switzerland. Over 800,000 DOIs have already been issued since 2009.

Do you run a document server, digital collection, research data archive or open-access journal?

Digital Object Identifiers:

DOI names remain stable and guarantee citability, while URL addresses frequently change or disappear.

We gladly offer you advice on introducing DOIs for your publication service. Further information on the procedure and contact details is available on the DOI Desk website.
