# asvz-to-feature-online-counter-for-courses-and-camps-as-of-may-2015.html

**Source:** en_internal/2015/03/asvz-to-feature-online-counter-for-courses-and-camps-as-of-may-2015.html

## Main article

The run on the ASVZ counters tends to be quite considerable courses and camps open for registration: those interested wait a long time in queues in the sports centres even before the counters open to secure one of the popular places. For that reason, a ticket system like that at the post office or SBB was rolled out at the Polyterrasse in recent years so that a fair process can be ensured.

The ASVZ will be offering an additional channel to register for courses and camps in its online counter as of May 2015. Anyone interested is recommended to prepare all the information necessary to register with the online counter early. Registrations are possible as of 30 March 2015.

Detailed information on the online counter can be found (in German) in the Message of the ASVZ as well as in the FAQs , which answer the key questions. To register with the online counter, please go to asvz.ch/schalter .

The Academic Sports Association Zurich (ASVZ) is as non-profit organisation. It provides a sports programme for all students, employees and alumni on behalf of ETH Zurich, the University of Zurich and the Zurich University of Applied Sciences (ZFH).
