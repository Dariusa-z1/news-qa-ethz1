## Main article

The " Best Practices in Digitization " issued by ETH-Bibliothek's DigiCenter offers practical tips and well-founded recommendations. The handbook covers the entire process from planning and realizing a digitization project to storage aspects and handling metadata.

The DigiCenter boasts longstanding expertise and a professional technical infrastructure. It has processed complex projects , digitized different document types and produced several millions of scans in recent years.
