The Fulbright Specialist Program allows you to invite an expert from the U.S. for a period of 2 to 6 weeks to your group to work with you to deliver workshops or seminars, develop academic curricula or training, lecture or conduct assessments, and build up long term academic relationships and collaboration

Your proposed program should start at the latest in fall 2024.

For more information see the instructions below or the Specialist Program website .
