## Main article

The Material Archive website offers a wealth of additional information on material description, properties, processing techniques and manufacturing methods. Moreover, photos illustrate traditional and innovative material patterns and application examples.

The collection is open to students and scientists from all disciplines and anyone else who is generally interested.

Want to find out more about Calanca, Juglans or Raku? Then browse the Material Archive or visit the Baubibliothek. Perhaps you will even discover a new, favourite material.
