## Main article

The six versions of the 2014 New Year card combine festive motifs with soft colours, modern designs and a touch of technical flair. One of the highlights of this year’s selection is an image showing the northern lights tracing the outline of a Christmas tree against a dark-blue sky. The New Year cards are designed and produced by the graphic design team in the ETH Zurich Printing Centre.

Members of ETH Zurich can purchase the 2014 New Year cards for 3 Swiss francs each (including an envelope). For an additional charge, you can now add a personal printed greeting too. The Printing Centre will accept orders up to and including 23 December. You can order cards over the counter at the printing centres on the Hönggerberg campus (HIL C45) and on the Zentrum campus (HG D48.2), by e-mail ( , , ) or online via the ETH Zurich Intranet .
