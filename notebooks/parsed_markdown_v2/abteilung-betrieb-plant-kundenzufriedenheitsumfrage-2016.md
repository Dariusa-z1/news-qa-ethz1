## Main article

In autumn 2016, we will once again be carrying out a survey in order to gauge the level of satisfaction amongst our customers.

The intention is to use the assessments, requirements and requests that emerge from this survey as a basis for developing carefully planned measures to boost our users’ trust and satisfaction in our services.
