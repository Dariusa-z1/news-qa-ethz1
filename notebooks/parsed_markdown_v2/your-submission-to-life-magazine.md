## Main article

Whether it's useful tips for work, a funny anecdote from life at ETH Zurich, or perhaps a closer look at a topic of interest, we're offering a platform for your thoughts in by Friday, 7 August 2020 . The editorial team will then select the most promising topic. If your idea is selected, please submit a short article of around 1,500 characters (including spaces) by 28 August 2020 . We look forward to receiving your suggestions!
