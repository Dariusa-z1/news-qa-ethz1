ZFF talk: "Can genius be taught?"

Life Science Zurich is organising talks around three documentaries at this year’s Zurich Film Festival (ZFF). In this series, ETH Professor Elsbeth Stern joins neuroscientist and chess player Monika Müller-Seps for the talk "Can genius be taught?" to explain what happens to the brain during the learning process and discuss new approaches to learning.

Friday, 30 September 2016, 3 pm - 4 pm

ZFF Festival Centre at Zurich’s Sechseläutenplatz
