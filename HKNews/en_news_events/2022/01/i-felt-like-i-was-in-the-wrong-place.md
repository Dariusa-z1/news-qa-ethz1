## Advice for students

In the first episode, computer science student Selin Baris talks about how she moved from Turkey to Zurich, how she got over her homesickness and how she learned to strike a balance between her studies and free time.

## Further information

The ETH Zurich offers advice centres for (virtually) any situation that may arise during your studies.
