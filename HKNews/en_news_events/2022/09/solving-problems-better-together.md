## Main article

ETH Week is a course that aims to foster collaboration, the adoption of an independent and critical mindset as well as the development of a sense of responsibility. Using the Design Thinking method, the students spent the week developing solutions and prototypes in interdisciplinary teams. In the video, they share their experience.
