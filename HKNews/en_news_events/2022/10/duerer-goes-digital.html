The Collection Catalogue Online

The Collection Catalogue Online with over 50,000 digitised artworks from the Graphische Sammlung is freely accessible.

Almost all works by Albrecht Dürer, Rembrandt and Pablo Picasso, for example, are now available online. Interested parties can also view new additions online, such as the extensive donation of drawings by Lucerne artist Max von Moos (1903–1979). The project is ongoing, with artworks being digitised on a continuous basis.
