# its-important-to-believe-in-yourself

## Advice for students

Lina De Windt experienced a completely new world when she began her studies at ETH. She came from a high school where sport – in her case ballet – was the main focus. At ETH she had to catch up in many subjects. In the video she talks about how she experienced this change and shares advice for new students.

## Further informationen

ETH Zurich offers advice centres for any situation that may arise during your studies.
