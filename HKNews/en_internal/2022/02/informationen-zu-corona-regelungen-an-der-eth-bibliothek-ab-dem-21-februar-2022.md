## Services

- The Reading Room Collections and Archives and the exhibition of the Max Frisch Archive are regularly open Mon–Fri from 10.00–17.00. For the Reading Room pre-registration is kindly requested.

- The Thomas Mann Archives are accessible by appointment. The exhibition of the Graphische Sammlung can be visited during regular opening hours Mon–Sun from 10.00–16.45.

Please note that a mask obligation applies at all ETH Library locations.

## Collections, archives and exhibitions

The regular opening hours apply to all locations. All services and offers can be used as usual.

## Events

Events are partly taking place on location again. A mask obligation applies. Please read the notes and details about upcoming events in the events calendar .

## Face masks mandatory

Face masks must be worn in all ETH buildings. This also applies to the study spaces. Please bear this in mind when visiting the ETH Library.
