In order to support high quality, future-proof education in Switzerland, the Swiss federal government encourages international cooperation in education in various forms. This online event by Movetia will provide a platform for lecturers, administrators and support officers to get to know

  • the project format “international cooperation” as supported by Movetia, mainly via project examples;
  • funding opportunities for educational cooperation (administered by Movetia)
  • peers within the HEIs in Zurich
  • contact and support persons within your institutions and the national funding agency Movetia.

The event is intended for lecturers and researchers, support staff and other potential project beneficiaries, including student associations at higher education institutions in Zurich.

Online event on Tuesday, 28 June 2022, 12.00-13.30.
