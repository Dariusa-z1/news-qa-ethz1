## In the city center and no plans yet?

In addition to test tubes, measuring instruments and reference books, the buildings of ETH Zurich also include 300 works of art worth seeing. You can now discover part of this ETH art inventory on the guided tour «Kunst zum Zmittag» in the pleasantly chilly main building. In just 60 minutes, you will explore the fascinating the fascination friction between exact sciences and ambiguous art.

## Always up to date

The guided tours will start every Friday in front of the poster display ’Kunst zum Zmittag‘ at the Polyterrasse (see photo). Take the chance and drop by spontaneously. An ETH guide will welcome you and all other spontaneous visitors punctually at 12.30 pm.

And if this week isn’t convenient: All dates for these and other guided tours at ETH can be found in the official calendar of events .
