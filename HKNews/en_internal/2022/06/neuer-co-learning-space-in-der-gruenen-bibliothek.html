A table, a bench, a stool, a stand or all at the same time. Since the beginning of June, the modular furniture system PIXEL has optimally complemented the learning environment in the Green Library on approx. 100 square metres. The handy wooden boxes can be converted in just a few steps. In this way, the room adapts to your current learning or teaching setting, whether for individual learning, group work or an event with up to 20 people.

Visit us in the Green Library and get to know the new co-learning space!

This redesign represents a further step in the paradigm shift in how we view the requirements for spaces and furniture at the ETH Library. Learning environments that can accommodate different learning situations will become more and more important at ETH Zurich.
