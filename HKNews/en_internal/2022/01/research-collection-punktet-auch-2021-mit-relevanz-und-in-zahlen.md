## Breaking records

Another interesting point to note is that the article with the highest altmetric score back in 2020 was on the subject of climate change. Despite the COVID-19 pandemic, nothing has changed in this regard for 2021. The research report itself also deals with the subject of setting new records – but in a more negative sense: “Increasing probability of record-shattering climate extremes” .

## Rankings

There is one doctoral thesis that has regularly topped the rankings since the statistics were first recorded: “Eine neue Bestimmung der Moleküldimensionen“ – the groundbreaking 17-page 1905 essay by one of ETH Zurich’s most famous alumni: Albert Einstein.

## Dominance

The high level of interest in this article is a strong indicator that the publication of research data is becoming increasingly important and that the publication volume of this type of publication is growing.
