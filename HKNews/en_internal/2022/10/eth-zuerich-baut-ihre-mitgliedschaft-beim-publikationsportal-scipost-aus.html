Since the start of ETH Zurich membership in 2020, the volume of publications by ETH researchers at external pageSciPostcall_made has risen steadily and ETH Zurich has meanwhile increased its financial support.

SciPost is an open-​access publication portal that is funded through university and institutional memberships rather than article processing charges (APC). Authors and readers can use its services free of charge. The portal was initially founded as a physics resource consisting of three journals, external pageSciPost Physics,call_made external pageSciPost Physics Proceedingscall_made and external pageSciPost Physics Lecture Notes.call_made Today, users of the portal can publish their research findings in several journals specialising in biology, chemistry, mathematics and physics.
