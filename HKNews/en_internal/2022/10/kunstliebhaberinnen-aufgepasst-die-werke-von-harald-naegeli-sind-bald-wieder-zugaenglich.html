The Department of Real Estate Management recently announced the planned reopening of the parking garage on 7 November 2022.

This is sure to be welcome news for all art lovers, as the reopening will once again allow access to the spray-painted artworks of Harald Naegeli located in this garage.

More information about these works and their preservation can be found in this ETHeritage blog article.
