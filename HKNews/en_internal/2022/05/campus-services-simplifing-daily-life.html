Campus Channels, Mobility and Signage are just three of the 16 service areas that Campus Services offers across ETH. To achieve top-level teaching and outstanding research, a finely honed infrastructure is indispensable. Time is the scarcest resource at the university, and as Prof. Ulrich Weidmann, Vice President for Infrastructure, explains: «Our professional services mean that the students and staff can focus on their main tasks – because we see to everything else.»

A new name and orientation

The development project launched by the Executive Board’s Infrastructure division specifically addressed future challenges. This involved streamlining departmental profiles and enhancing interfaces between ETH organisational units. As well as a change of name from Services to Campus Services, the department also underwent structural changes. Campus Services now focuses more keenly on its core area of expertise: providing holistic and integrative on-site services across ETH. The various service portfolios are managed by four sections: Events and Hospitality, Information and Experience, Mobility and Logistics and Print and Publish, which are all assisted by the Management and Support section. The ETH Platform for Workshop Technology, previously part of the Services department, joined the newly created Engineering and Systems department in January 2022 (cf. Intern aktuell, 21.09.2021).

Services for every situation

The Campus Services offering is as diverse as the university and the staff themselves. This, believes Stephanie Braunwalder, Head of Campus Services, is one of the department’s strengths: «Our services are tailored to the needs of our clients. Because it’s our job to see that great things are achieved and recognised at ETH, while everyday things run smoothly. We strive to make things easier for those experiencing, working and living at ETH.» Ongoing discussion and contact with the various university stakeholders and the entire ETH community mean that her department is able to convey to the public an authentic impression of ETH Zurich: «Our Visitors’ packages feature guided and theme tours that give a gripping insight into what goes on at ETH, and vividly bring the world of science to life.»
