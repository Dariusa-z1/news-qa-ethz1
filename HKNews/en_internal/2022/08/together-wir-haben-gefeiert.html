This is the PeKo

The Staff Commission acts as a contact point for technical and administrative ETH staff and represents their concerns to the Executive Board. The diverse work of the commission includes participation in the University Assembly, the Strategy Commission, the Catering Commission, in consultations and in proposals to the Executive Board.
To achieve its goals, the PeKo relies on dialogue and cooperation.
