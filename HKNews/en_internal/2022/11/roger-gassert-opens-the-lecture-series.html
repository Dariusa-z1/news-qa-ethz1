In the RESC online lecture series, speakers from the network of the ETH competence centre Rehabilitation Engineering and Science (RESC) provide insights into key topics in rehabilitation - from care to research to personal experiences.

In the first edition, Roger Gassert, Professor of Rehabilitation Engineering and Head of RELab at ETH Zurich, will share his own personal journey as a patient and identify major challenges in rehabilitation as well as milestones along his path to recovery.
