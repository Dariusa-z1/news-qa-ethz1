Two out of three stores to be operated by Orell Füssli

From 1 January 2023, Orell Füssli Thalia AG will take over the ETH Store on the Hönggerberg campus and the branch on the Polyterrasse. The second ETH Store in the Centre – the branch in Sonneggstrasse – will continue to be operated by ETH Store AG and from February 2023 be managed as a new flagship store for ETH merchandise and other services and products.

Orell Füssli will take over the staff of both ETH Stores and the existing inventory. This is to be supplemented by selected specialist literature, non-fiction books and study materials.

New online shop, better conditions

ETH students and staff will be able to access the online shop of external pageDelivros Orell Füsslicall_made, one of Switzerland’s biggest operators in the procurement of printed and digital media for schools, businesses, the authorities and libraries. Via this online shop, they will also have access to the entire Orell Füssli product range. The merchandise currently offered by the ETH Store will be taken over in the new online shop.

Customers will benefit from better conditions, a large and established distribution network (shipping, pick-up, etc.) and enhanced online functions. Current services, vouchers and other advantages (student discount, etc.) will remain unaffected.
