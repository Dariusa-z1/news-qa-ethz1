## Main article

Our online portal gives speedy help and guidance on:

• Goods transport: planning, coordinating and executing transport
• Post: distribution of internal and external post
• Warehouse: storage and retrieval of goods
• Consumables: office materials and recycling
• Parking at ETH: parking options, prices and application forms
• ETH vehicles: vehicle maintenance and fuel management
The Logistics Help Centre brings together all the online forms, documents and leaflets for the range of logistics services provided by Campus Services. In just a few clicks, you’ll have answers to your questions, and the information you need for using a particular service.

## Interactive search function

Simply click the Help button and type your question or keyword there. Straight away, an interactive Answer Bot will supply the relevant search results.The Answer Bot is on hand around the clock, and constantly learning! The Help Centre not only provides the right information, just when you need it – it also assists our Logistics team. Which means we can take care of your requests even faster.

## Advantages of the Logistics Help Centre

• all relevant information at a glance

• interactive Answer Bot 24/7
• shorter processing time
• supports Logistics team with requests

## Does the Help Centre replace the ETH Logistics website?

You’ll still find basic information on Campus Services’ logistics services on the Logistics and Transport website. The Logistics Help Centre rounds out the information on the website and has additional features. At one go, it compiles all the relevant online forms, documents and factsheets. The Logistics Help Centre and all logistics services are offered by Campus Services.
