# call-for-proposal-for-conferences

**Source:** en_internal/2017/07/call-for-proposal-for-conferences.html

## Main article

The Congressi Stefano Franscini, ETH Zurich's conference platform since 1989, gives the possibility to organise research conferences at Monte Verità, a hilltop over Ascona and Lake Maggiore. All researchers and university professors working in Switzerland are invited to submit their proposal for a meeting to be held in 2019; international collaborations are welcome.

Deadline for submitting the applications 2019: January 15, 2018. The application form can be downloaded from the CSF online platform at the address: www.csf.ethz.ch .

Furthermore, the CSF gives ETH Zurich researchers or professors the possibility to organise a Winter School for doctoral students at Monte Verità, a hilltop over Ascona and Lake Maggiore. The aim is to provide participants with in depth knowledge on a specific research topic and insight into its leading edge research.
