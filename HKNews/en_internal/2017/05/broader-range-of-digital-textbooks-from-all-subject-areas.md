## Main article

For mathematics students:

• Analysis 1 by G.B. Thomas,
• Probability and Statistics by M.H. DeGroot
• Topology by J. Munkres
For people interested in biology:

• Brock Biology of Microorganisms or
• Campbell Biology
For students of the economics sciences the following are now available online:

• Human Resource Management by G. Dessler,
• S.P. Robbins' Organizational Behavior
• Principles of Marketing by P. Kotler
Other essential textbooks include:

• Political Science: An Introduction by M.G. Roskin,
• Intellectual Property by D. Bainbridge
• The interdisciplinary title Writing Research Papers: A Complete Guide by J.D. Lester, a companion for students from the choice of topic to the finished document
