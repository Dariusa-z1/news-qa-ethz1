# intensive-german-for-doctoral-students

**Source:** en_internal/2017/05/intensive-german-for-doctoral-students.html

## Main article

The course will include the following two parts:

• Two-week intensive course: 14 - 25 August 2017 and 15 - 26 January 2018 Monday to Friday from 9:15 - 12:45 and 13:45 - 15:15 (60 lessons)
• Semester course of two double lessons per week: from 19 Sept. - 12 Dec. 2017 and 20 Feb. - 31 May 2018 Tuesday and Thursday from 18:15 - 20:00 (56 lessons)
From zero to Level B1 in one year - doctoral students will obtain enough German competencies within 12 months to be able to communicate in German both in connection with their work and socially. The registration is for the full one-year course, and once submitted, it is binding. The course must be paid in full (CHF 2’650.–) at the time of registration. The tertiary institute will support course participants in the current pilot phase with a substantial financial contribution (one third by rectorate, one third possibly by professor and one third by the participant).

Register online from 29 May to 13 July 2017 and find further information at www.sprachenzentrum.ethz.ch
