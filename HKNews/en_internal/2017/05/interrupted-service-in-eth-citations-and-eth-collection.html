ETH Library will launch the Research Collection in the middle of June 2017, a new platform for academic publications and research data from ETH Zurich.

The two applications ETH E-Citations and ETH E-Collection will be merged into the new publication platform.

To ensure that the migration goes smoothly, it will not be possible to enter new publications in ETH E-Citations or publish via ETH E-Collection from 15 May to 12 June. Nonetheless, both platforms will remain fully functional for searches and access to documents already published.  

If you need to submit a Doctoral or Habilitation Thesis during this period, please contact us when your file is ready to be uploaded. We will provide you access to an alternative submission process.

Further information about the new Research Collection will be sent out by mid-June.

Please do not hesitate to contact us at if you have any questions.
