The Swiss National Science Foundation SNSF has published guidelines for Data Management Plans. As of October 2017 researchers are requested to submit a Data Management Plan as an integral part of their research application.

What does this mean for researchers at ETH Zurich? What kind of requirements do they have to comply with in the future?

Thursday 6 July 2017

Zentrum, Room HG E 1.1: 13.00 to 14.00
Hönggerberg, Room HPV G 5: 17.00 to 18.00

Registration is not necessary. The event will be held in English.

More information on the topic of Data Management Plans can be found on the Digital Curation website.
