# schnupperstudium-winter-2017-is-open-for-registration

**Source:** en_internal/2017/01/schnupperstudium-winter-2017-is-open-for-registration.html

## Main article

Participants will spend three days on campus at ETH, and will learn to program and write their own Android apps, immerse in different areas of computer science, will attend lectures by ETH lecturers and meet computer science students. They will learn about their career options via a visit to the local industry.

The upcoming Schnupperstudium will take place on February 13-15th, 2017 on the ETH main Campus. The participation is free. All instruction is in German.

Please register at: http://www.frauen.inf.ethz.ch/fuer-schuelerinnen/schnupperstudium.html Places are limited.

Schnupperstudium Informatik is organized by CSNOW, the Network of Women in Computer Science. It takes place twice a year, in February and September. Visit the website for more information: http://www.frauen.inf.ethz.ch
