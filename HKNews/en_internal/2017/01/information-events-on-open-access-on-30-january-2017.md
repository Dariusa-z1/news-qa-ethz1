## Main article

Programme

• Introduction: Professor Ulrich Weidmann, Vice-President of Human Resources and Infrastructure, ETH Zurich
• Presentation: What is open access? Current situation and trends in academic publishing, relevance for ETH Zurich (Dr Rafael Ball, Director ETH-Bibliothek)
• Q&A session
• Open Access survey in February
ETH-Bibliothek will be conducting a survey on open access in February 2017. On 1 February, all academic staff members at ETH Zurich will receive a personal link to the online questionnaire via email.

Beforehand, however, ETH-Bibliothek is holding two information events, which anyone interested is more than welcome to attend:

Date Monday 30 January 2017

Venue Hönggerberg, HPV G 5: 12:00 – 1:00 p.m. Zentrum, HG E 3: 5:00 – 6:00 p.m.

The event will be held in English. There is no need to register.
