Would you like to play an active role in promoting staff interests?

The ETH Zurich Personnel Committee (PeKo) is looking for motivated administrative or technical staff at ETH who are keen to get involved in personnel issues to join the committee for the 2015-2018 term.

PeKo represents the interests of administrative and technical staff to the Executive Board of ETH Zurich and participates in dealing with current issues relating to personnel and university policy at ETH.

If you are interested in joining PeKo and willing to invest your time in it over the next four years, then we would be happy to receive your application.

All employees of ETH Zurich who have been working for ETH for at least six months on an ongoing and permanent basis and with an employment level of at least 50% FTE are eligible to apply. The term of office will begin in April 2015. The members elected to PeKo for the next four-year term will be informed personally.

Have we sparked your interest? If so, please send us your application documents – including your personal details, a photo, a brief CV, details of your place of work and position and a covering letter – to the Head of the Election Office by 12 January 2015 at the following address:

Mr Cuno Künzler, Interim Head of Human Resources,
Turnerstrasse 1,
ETH-Zentrum,
CH-8092 Zurich.

Please state the reference: “PeKo elections”.
