## Main article

The Congressi Stefano Franscini offers:

• a substantial financial support (provided by: ETH Zurich and Swiss National Science Foundation)
• administrative assistance in the organization and carrying out of your meeting
• a modern conference centre in a magnificent venue, very interesting from the historical and cultural point of view, with a hotel and a restaurant on site
• the CSF Award: a prize to be assigned to young researchers for the best oral presentation
The Congressi Stefano Franscini asks for:

• the organization of a scientifically top-quality conference lasting 3 to 5 days
• the participation of an international scientific audience composed of 50-100 senior and junior researchers, staying at Monte Verità for the whole duration of the conference
All researchers and university professors working in Switzerland are invited to submit their proposal for a meeting or an ETH Winter School to be held in 2016; international collaborations are welcome.

The applications will be evaluated by an interdisciplinary scientific board.

The application form can be downloaded from the CSF online platform .

Deadline for submitting the applications 2016: January 15, 2015
