In providing real-world examples of environmental, financial, and social successes, the International Alliance of Research Universities (IARU) hopes to inspire innovation and creative action in universities around the globe. ETH Zurich in collaboration with ten of the world's top research universities – the IARU network launched this week the «Green Guide for Universities – IARU Pathways Towards Sustainability.» It includes 23 inspiring cases that have made a difference at IARU universities around the world.

Interested in learning more? Download a free copy of the Green Guide for Universities and benefit from the challenges and opportunities faced by IARU member institutions in making campuses sustainable.
