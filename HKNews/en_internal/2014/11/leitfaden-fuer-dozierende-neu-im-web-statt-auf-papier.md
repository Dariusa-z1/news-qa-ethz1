## Guidelines for Lecturers

Anyone who lectures at ETH Zurich will be familiar with the orange-coloured Guidelines for Lecturers document. Until now, the Rectorate has sent copies of these guidelines to every new lecturer by mail.

## Subscribe to Newsletter

The Rectorate has now made them available online. There are clear benefits to this: not only are the guidelines now accessible from anywhere, but they can be amended quickly too.

## Staffnet

These guidelines, which are geared towards the procedures members of our faculty follow to help them plan and teach their courses, arrange topics according to various process steps.

The Guidelines can be found here:

www.ethz.ch/guidelines-lecturers
