# fire-protection-course

## Main article

Each student will have the opportunity to fight a fire with a portable fire extinguisher under expert guidance.

The course is free for all interested members of ETH. For some undergraduate students engaging in certain fields of study, participation is mandatory in the first semester. They will be informed by e-mail.

The courses will last just over one hour. They will start on 22 September and will be carried out up to 17 October 2014. The participants can travel by public transport to the station "ETH Hönggerberg". From there, the course area can be reached by a short walk (about 10 minutes). There are no parking lots at the course site.

The course will take place outdoor and in all weather conditions. Therefore sturdy boots and waterproof clothing are essential.

If you are interested, please register on the website .
