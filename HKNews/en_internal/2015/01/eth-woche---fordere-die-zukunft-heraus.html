Food: Healthy. Fair. Enough for All.

The purpose of the ETH Week is to foster critical thinking skills and to motivate students of all departments for sustainable action through an innovative learning experience.

The topic of the first ETH Week is as complex as it is relevant: the state of the global food supply in the 21st century. Addressing it requires ecological, agricultural, and technical knowledge that is contextualized by an awareness of complex political, economic, and societal systems.

Place: 
ETH Zürich Hönggerberg

Date: 
Sunday 6.9.2015 to Friday 11.9.2015, daily full-time programme.

Language:
English

Who may apply?
Bachelor’s degree students who will complete their 4th or 6thsemester by September 6-11, 2015 may submit an application for the ETH Week.

Call for Applications: Deadlines
Application will be open from mid-February, 2015
Deadline: April 18, 2015

Notification of acceptance to participants: April 30, 2015 at latest.
