## Main article

ETH-Bibliothek will be giving the following talks at the Open Access Days:

Monday, 7 September, 16.15, session 1: (Linked) Open Data and Cultural Heritage Nicole Graf (Image Archive): from the image agency to an open data supplier: realignment of the licensing model of ETH-Bibliothek’s Image Archive.

Tuesday, 8 September, 10.45 , session 5: Data Repositories and Open Access Matthias Töwe (Digital Curation): research data and open access: organisation is… part and parcel of it. Barbara Hirschmann (E-Publishing): development of standards and best practices in the field of research data publication. A glance at DataCite's work.

Tuesday, 8 September,14.00, session 10: News for Librarians Nadja Böller (Consortium of Swiss Academic Libraries): "Access to everything for everyone – forever." How the "Nationallizenzen" project supports the Swiss open-access movement.
