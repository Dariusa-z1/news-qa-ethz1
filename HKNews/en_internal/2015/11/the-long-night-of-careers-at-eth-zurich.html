The "Long Night of the Careers (LNoC)" will take place this year for the second time on the 12th of November at ETH Zurich. This event is aimed at students, doctorates, post-docs and alumni, and will be held in the ETH main building. Visitors can take advantage of an exceptional platform to meet former ETH students, company representatives and like-minded people in a direct and casual environment.

In 2015, the "Long Night of the Careers (LNoC)" is being held at 12 universities and university-level institutions.

Further information www.ethz.ch/lnoc.
