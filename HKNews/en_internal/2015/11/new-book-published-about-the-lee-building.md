## New public pathway

The new LEE Building at ETH Zurich is situated very close to the main building, forming part of the Zurich skyline and making it a prominent example of urban design. The exceptional plans by architect Fawad Kazi broke new ground from a design perspective. The newly published book, ‘ETH Zürich Building LEE’, focuses on three main aspects: urban integration, construction and fabrication of the concrete elements, and comprehensive documentation of the construction process through plans and images.

## Further information

Essays on various topics are supplemented by short texts that illuminate various aspects of the LEE Building. This unique book provides an in-depth picture of both the seemingly straight-forward and complex aspects of the construction of this building. This is rounded off with texts written by the architects, the builders, and the planners.

## Subscribe to Newsletter

With the construction of the LEE Building, the city of Zurich now has a new footpath: Leonhardsteig. This pathway is publicly accessible and serves as a continuation of the road from Central along Auf der Maur to Clausiusstrasse, along the LEE’s northern facade.
