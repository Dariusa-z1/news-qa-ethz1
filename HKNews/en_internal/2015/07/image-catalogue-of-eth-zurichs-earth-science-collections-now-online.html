The materials stem from the Albert Heim Collection, which was gathered from all over the world between the 1880s and the 1910s.

The catalogue can be browsed according various search terms, such as fossils, sediments, volcanic products or metamorphic rocks. The digitized objects can be viewed three-dimensionally and downloaded free of charge.

Further information is available at ETH E-Pics Earth Science Collections
