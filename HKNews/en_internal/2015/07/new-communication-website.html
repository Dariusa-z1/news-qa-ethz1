Do you need report templates in the university’s corporate design, or guidelines on using logos or creating a social media presence? Or perhaps you would like to get in touch with our media relations team or arrange for individualised consulting services. The new communication website www.ethz.ch/communication brings together the most important information and contact details you need for all your communications work.

What can you find on the new website?

  • Communications consulting how to get in touch with the Corporate Communications consultants who can advise you on your communications needs.
  • Media work information and advice on working with the media.
  • ETH identity an overview of official templates, logos, guidelines and documents that can help you with your day-to-day communications work.
  • Corporate Design implementation guidelines for office materials and creative design.
  • Websites how to create, edit and migrate new and existing sites.
  • Social media tips and tricks for creating and maintaining a social media presence.
  • Video guidelines on film, video and multimedia projects.
  • Key information about ETH includes the standard brief profile text and the ETH presentation (PPT).
