Whenever possible, the digital images in ETH-Bibliothek’s Image Archive can be downloaded for free in web resolution (1024x768) and as high-resolution JPGs and TIFFs. The images can be used freely for scientific, private, non-commercial and commercial purposes.

You can find further information on ETH E-Pics Image Archive Online.
