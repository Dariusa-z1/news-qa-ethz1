Two buses run back and forth between ETH Zurich’s Zentrum and Hönggerberg campuses on every weekday, making a total of 69 trips per day. These vehicles have now been given a makeover.  

One of the buses has been sporting the new design since 26 May, with the second due to follow in mid-June. The ETH Link is run by Eurobus and transports almost 2,000 people on an average day.
