## Main article

Newly elected prorector:

• Professor Paolo Ermanni has been named as the new Prorector for Continuing Education. The 55-year-old from Ticino has been a full Professor of Structure Technologies at ETH Zurich since 1 April 2003. He is also Director of the Congressi Stefano Franscini.
Re-elected prorectors:

• Professor Joachim Buhmann , Prorector for Study Programmes. Professor Buhmann has been a full Professor of Information Science and Engineering at ETH Zurich since October 2003.
• Professor Thomas Vogel , Prorector for Doctoral Studies. Professor Vogel has been a full Professor of Structural Engineering at ETH Zurich’s Institute of Structural Engineering since 1 October 1995.
• Professor Andreas Vaterlaus , Prorector for Curriculum Development. Professor Vaterlaus has been a full Professor of Physics and Education at ETH Zurich since August 2008.
