# eth-zurichs-2014-annual-report-is-now-available

## Main article

ETH Zurich continued to grow in 2014. By the end of the year, some 18,500 men and women in total had studied at ETH Zurich during the period under review – more than ever before.

This growth is also reflected in ETH Zurich’s financial figures. The university’s total expenditure reached CHF 1,556 million in 2014, an increase of 2.9 percent on the previous year.

You can find all this information and more about ETH Zurich in the 2014 Annual Report, which has just been published by the Executive Board. ETH Zurich’s Annual Report contains short features, attractive photographs and charts showing the most significant achievements, developments and events of the past year.
