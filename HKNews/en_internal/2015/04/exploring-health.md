## Main article

How can we stay healthy, and how can we keep our families healthy? The Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention Institute’s research, teaching, and services improve the health of communities in Zurich and beyond. Members of ETH Zurich and the general public are invited to visit the institute on 6 June for 29 fun and interactive activities where they can learn about the institute’s activities – and the state of their own health. The event also includes an engaging children’s programme.

Learn the latest in health research and practice from our own scientists and guest speakers like Councillor Dr. Thomas Heiniger, journalist Sara Hildebrand (health program Pulse , formerly of Glanz & Gloria ) and Prof. Dr. Nino Künzli (Director of the Swiss School of Public Health). Comedian and physician Fabian Unteregger will moderate the talks.

Stop by on 6 June and enjoy knowledge, fun, and food for the whole family!

"Der Gesundheit auf der Spur”

Saturday, 6 June 2015, 11am – 4pm

Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Prevention Institute

Hirschgraben 84, 8001 Zurich, near Central tram stop
