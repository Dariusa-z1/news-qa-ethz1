# a-moment-of-silence-for-garissa

**Source:** en_internal/2015/04/a-moment-of-silence-for-garissa.html

## Main article

ETH President Lino Guzzella and Rector Sarah Springman will show their solidarity with the victims of the terror attack on Garissa University College in Kenya by taking a stand against attacks on freedom in education and research. They will join the European University Association in one minute of silence at noon on 27 April and invite you to do the same.
