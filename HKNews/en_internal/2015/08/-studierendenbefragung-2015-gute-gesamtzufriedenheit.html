In spring 2015, ETH Zurich conducted its first ever survey among Bachelor’s and Master’s students to ask how satisfied they were with their studies at the university. The 2015 Student Survey of Satisfaction found that 82 per cent of students at ETH Zurich are satisfied or very satisfied with their studies.

The Executive Board of ETH Zurich acknowledged the 2015 Student Survey of Satisfaction at its meeting on 3 July, 2015. Both the associated working group and the Executive Board classified the results as good overall. The Executive Board determined that the areas identified as needing some more work were already very well covered by various initiatives that are already underway.
