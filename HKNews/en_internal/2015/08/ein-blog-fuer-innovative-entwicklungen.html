In the Deliscope blog educational developers describe and publish 'best-practice' examples of teaching and inform about trends in innovative teaching at ETH Zurich.

Educational developers are contact persons in the departments for issues in the area of teaching and learning. They provide support to lecturers and coordinate developments in cooperation with the Educational Development and Technology (LET) unit. They are in direct contact with the faculty in their departments and thus have direct knowledge of lecturers' specific needs in their teaching activities.
