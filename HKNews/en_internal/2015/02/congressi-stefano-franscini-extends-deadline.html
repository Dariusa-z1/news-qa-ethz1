The Congressi Stefano Franscini (CSF), the meeting platform of the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology of Zurich (ETH Zurich), has extended the deadline for submission of Proposals for obtaining a subsidy from the CSF for conferences in 2016  from 15 January 2015 to 8 February 2015.

Conferences 2016 Congressi Stefano Franscini

The Congressi Stefano Franscini (CSF) gives the possibility to organize research conferences at Monte Verità, a hilltop over Ascona and Lake Maggiore.

All researchers and university professors working in Switzerland are invited to submit their proposal for a meeting to be held
in 2016; international collaborations are welcome. The applications will be evaluated by an interdisciplinary scientific board.

The application form can be downloaded from our on line platform at the address: www.csf.ethz.ch.

ETH Winter Schools @Monte Verità 2015/2016

The Congressi Stefano Franscini (CSF) gives ETH Zurich researchers or professors the possibility to organize a Winter School for doctoral students at Monte Verità, a hilltop over Ascona and Lake Maggiore.

The aim is to provide participants with in depth knowledge on a specific research topic and insight into its leading edge research. The proposals will be evaluated by the Scientific Board of the CSF.

The application form can be downloaded from the CSF on line platform at www.csf.ethz.ch.
