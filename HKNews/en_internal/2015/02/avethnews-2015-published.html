At the beginning of each year, AVETH (Academic Association of Scientific Staff at ETH Zurich) issues the AVETH, the annual report of AVETH. Here, AVETH informs on the activities and events of the past year and provides and outlook for the new year.

Apart from the introduction of the various working group of AVETH, also the department associations are presented. The department associations are the organizations of scientific staff on department level and provide valuable work by creating networks within the scientific staff as well as doing the representation in the political processes.

Apart from the printed version, which was send to all members of the scientific staff of ETH, the AVETHnews are also electronically available through the website of AVETH.
