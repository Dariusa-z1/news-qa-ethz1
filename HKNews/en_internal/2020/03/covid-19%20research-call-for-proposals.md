# covid-19%20research-call-for-proposals

**Source:** en_internal/2020/03/covid-19%20research-call-for-proposals.html

## Main article

The Centre invites researchers from its four partner institutions (ETH Zurich, University of Basel, University Children’s Hospital Basel and Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute) to submit projects that can deliver impact in the COVID-19 pandemic.

The research areas to be considered are diagnostics for COVID-19, human immune response to COVID-19 and medical interventions and disease management for COVID-19.

Applications must be led by at least one principal investigator from the partner institutions. Projects are expected to be 2.5 years in duration and range from 0.5 to 2.5 Mio CHF. Deadline to submit is Wednesday April 8th.

Details of the call for proposals and application are available at brc.ch/ftc .
