## Main article

For some ETH members, spending time in their home office is a good opportunity for further training. For this purpose, our subject specialists have made a wide selection of options for you:

• There is hardly any topic on which there is no e-book in our inventory. Let yourself be inspired by a selection of e-book providers.
• Various scientific initiatives such as Folding@home, where you can make your computing power available to COVID-19 research, and platforms such as JoVe Science Education with explanatory videos are of particular interest during the current pandemic.
• MOOCs, open mass online courses, offer an opportunity to learn about a topic of your choice in the form of video lectures.
• Finally, we also have a few tips on how to distract yourself from the ubiquitous coronavirus. Did you know the SUVA movement tips for people who spend a large portion of the day sitting, for example, or the digital Swiss World Atlas, which makes it easy to dream of distant countries?
