## Main article

The funding covers open access fees for articles in Gold open access journals and in Hybrid journals. The research depicted in funded articles has to be related to the current crises. This includes all aspects related to the COVID-19 pandemics such as medical, economic, policy and social studies.

The requirement for funding is for the first or corresponding author of the article to be a member of ETH Zurich.

To apply for funding, please send us an e-mail with information on the article to before submitting it. We will then decide whether we consider the article for funding and inform you about further steps.

You can find information on further open access funding options on our webpage.
