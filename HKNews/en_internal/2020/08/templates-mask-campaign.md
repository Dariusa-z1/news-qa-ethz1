## Main article

From Monday, August 24, it will be "Mask on!" for everyone who is on the move in ETH Zurich buildings. This is to keep the risk of infection on campus as low as possible in view of the continuing high number of cases.

In the last few days, the implementation of visual communication had to be carried out with corresponding speed. From Monday, August 24th, the compulsory wearing of masks will be unmissable: Everywhere on campus, posters and screens will inform ETH members and visitors about it.

ETH units who wish to print the posters themselves and use their screens can download the corresponding templates in various formats from the ETH Zurich Corona webpage. All posters can also be ordered from the Print & Publish webshop.
