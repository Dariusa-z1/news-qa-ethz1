Do you work and conduct research in the area of applied biosciences? Would you like to have instant access to information on all the aspects of a particular subject area? The six CABI Compendia cover external pageanimal health and productioncall_made, external pageaquaculturecall_made, external pagecrop protectioncall_made, external pageforestrycall_made, external pagehorticulturecall_made and external pageinvasive speciescall_made. They offer an encyclopaedic resource which brings together a wide range of materials, including thematic journal articles from various publishers, book chapters, reports, case studies, fact sheets, images and (distribution) maps, which now feature a zoom function.

CABI works with a wide range of specialist organisations to compile the Compendia and update them on an ongoing basis, ensuring each edition includes the latest information from expert sources. If you need support in your studies, your research or any practical application, the Compendia are the ideal resource.

The CABI Compendia can be accessed via the ETH Zurich network.
