In the new webshop category “Emergency operation – Communication equipment”, you’ll find finished communication materials as well as templates for filling out. These can be used, for example, to create door signs for meeting rooms giving the maximum number of persons or hygiene instructions. Simply place your production order and have the material delivered to an ETH office or directly to your home address, free of charge. It’s as easy as that!

Our range of safety signage will comprise

  • FOPH flyers
  • Guidelines
  • Floor markers
  • Posters
  • Door signs
  • Notices
  • Access details
  • Info cards
  • Checklists

Haven't found what you’re looking for? Then please get in touch, and our Print and Publish specialists will go through design to finished product for your specific need.

Please bear in mind that we’ve reduced the number of employees on site to comply with FOPH measures, so delivery times may be longer than usual.

The new offering from Services has been developed to help ETH Zurich members cope with the current communication challenge. All our processes, including logistics, are geared to emergency conditions. Deliveries to private addresses (your home office) and to ETH buildings via internal mail are therefore free of charge until further notice.

The Print and Publish team are happy to respond to requests, suggestions or feedback on the new offering.
