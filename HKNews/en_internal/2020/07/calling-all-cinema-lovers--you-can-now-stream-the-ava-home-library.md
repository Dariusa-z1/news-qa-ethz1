# calling-all-cinema-lovers--you-can-now-stream-the-ava-home-library

## Main article

Have you ever dreamed of being programme director, audience member and jury member at the same time? Film aficionados now have something to look forward to during a time when they have been unable to watch films at the cinema – you can put on your very own film festival and make that dream come true at home! In cooperation with AVA, ETH Zurich is now offering a huge selection of ambitious cinema. The AVA streaming service specialises in European and international art-house films. In order to provide cinema fans with enough material to keep them going during the coronavirus crisis, the AVA Home Library is offering users the chance to stream a selection of around 200 films from the Documentary, Feature Film and Short Film categories until the end of the year. There are also a number of exclusive film collections to discover, including the Berlinale Collection, the Cannes Collection, Focus: LGBTQ and Focus: Africa. A collection of French-language films is also available. The AVA Home Library can be accessed via the ETH Zurich network or at the ETH Library until 28 December 2020.

#KnowMore – The prepared content issued by the ETH Library is at your free disposal and gives you a head start.
