## Main article

ETH members now have access to all journal titles on ScienceDirect . This involves a total of 16 million publications from 2,570 journals.

This is one of the results of the agreement negotiated by swissuniversities and the Consortium of Swiss University Libraries with Elsevier.

This agreement also regulates free open-access publishing in Elsevier journals for articles submitted from 2020 onwards. The prerequisite for this is that the corresponding author of the submitted article is employed at ETH Zurich. When submitting the manuscript, the ETH email address must be used.
