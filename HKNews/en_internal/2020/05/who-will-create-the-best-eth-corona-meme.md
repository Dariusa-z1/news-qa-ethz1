# who-will-create-the-best-eth-corona-meme

## Main article

Working and studying at home can be challenging. However, as complicated and unfamiliar as the current situation may be, with a little bit of humour, many things can be mastered more easily.

We're all familiar with the internet memes that make us smile, by combining images and text. For the new issue of the "life" magazine, we are now looking for the best ETH coronavirus memes. Let your creativity flow and send your meme to or post it on Instagram with the hashtag #ethzmemes.
