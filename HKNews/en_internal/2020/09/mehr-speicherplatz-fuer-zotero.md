## Newly licensed for you: Zotero Institution

Do you have a chaotic mess of PDFs, all saved in different places? Did you forget the name of that red book that you borrowed recently? Then you need reference management software. This software makes it easier to compile and organise PDFs and references. ETH provides access to various reference management software programmes, and the ETH Library offers advice and training when it comes to choosing the right software for you and using it to manage your references.

Zotero is a free, open-source software that not only helps you during the research process, but can also save a lot of time when it comes to citing. You can transfer the necessary information to the programme with one click, integrate PDFs via drag and drop, and create a bibliography with just one more click. The programme is browser-based and can be used with Windows, macOS and Linux. Thanks to the new licence for Zotero Institution, now the whole ETH Zurich faculty and all staff and students have access to unlimited storage space.
