The lecture will present largely unknown audio and film material and FBI information regarding the Mann family, retracing the trail blazed by its members in the USA in the face of diverse hostilities.

Tuesday, 19 November 2019, 18.00, in the ETH main building, Room E 1.2

Event language: German  

The event is part of the supporting programme to the exhibition “external pageThomas Mann in Amerikacall_made” at Zurich’s Strauhof in collaboration with the Thomas Mann Archives at ETH Zurich.
