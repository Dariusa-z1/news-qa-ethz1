# neues-normenportal

## Main article

The following standards are available on the new portal eresearchcenter :

• ASTM International • DIN • Electrosuisse • IEEE International • INB/Swissmem • ISO • SAE International • SIA International • VDI

For the first time, this enables the majority of the standards licensed at ETH Library to be used on a common platform. Moreover, unlike the previous Perinorm, the installation of the Adobe FileOpen plug-in is no longer required.
