## Main article

Our tours answer questions you might not even have thought of yet:

• How are assertions on the distant past made based on molecules and air bubbles in the Arctic ice?
• Which academic contexts do the many artworks on display in ETH Zurich’s main building refer to?
• What nooks and crannies of the main ETH Zurich building or Hönggerberg campus are you unaware of?
Find the answers to these and many more questions on the public tours of ETH Zurich’s Collections and Archives and the Services Department.

The free public tours are conducted on Tuesdays from 18:15 to 19:15.
