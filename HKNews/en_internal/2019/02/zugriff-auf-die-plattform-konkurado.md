## Main article

As of now, members of ETH Zurich have access to konkurado , an independent public and private procurement information platform in Switzerland for past, present and future architecture and engineering competitions. Besides announcing competitions and offers, konkurado also serves as a building culture archive and documents overs 2,000 competitions and procedures dating back to 1937. The archive is constantly being expanded.

Access to the current announcements is possible only for members of ETH Zurich and requires registration using the nethz login (Shibboleth/SWITCHaai). The archive is open to the public.
