# gesucht-ideen-fuer-werbeartikel-mit-mehrwert

## Main article

We are on the lookout for ideas for commercially available products and freebies to be distributed during courses, events or ETH Zurich campaigns (e.g. fresher bags).

The contest is open until 25 February 2019. Draw inspiration from the ideas already submitted and submit your own ideas at www.library.ethz.ch/ideas !

The winners receive a voucher of their choice from ETH Store, Projekt Neptun, SBB or Swiss Book Vouchers worth up to CHF 100.

ETH Library is looking forward to your ideas!
