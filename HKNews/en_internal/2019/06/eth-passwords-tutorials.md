Since April 15, 2019, the look & feel of the administration of passwords for ETH members has changed. The ETH Web Center is now available. www.password.ethz.ch ( Web Center Login ) In the Web Center, ETH members change their initial password, manage their passwords and store their authentication data when they log on. If ETH members have forgotten their password, they will receive a new password using the mobile phone number or alternative (non-ETH) email address stored there.
