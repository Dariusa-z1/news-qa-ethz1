Already some time has passed since the event “Persistent Identifiers in Research – Celebrating 10 Years of DOI Desk at ETH Zurich” took place on 13 September 2019. Speakers from different fields of expertise talked about the various aspects of persistent identifiers: from current applications, developments to future initiatives.

The slides from the event are now available and downloadable for everyone via the ETH Research Collection:

  • 10 Years DOI Desk at ETH Zurich, Barbara Hirschmann
  • Connecting Research, FAIR Data Practices, Britta Dreyer
  • Building Open Research Infrastructure with PIDs, Gabriela Mejias
  • Persistent Identifiers for Scientific Data at CSCS, Mario Valle
  • Persistent Identifiers and the openBIS Research Data Management System, Henry Lütcke
  • Tracking citations to research software via PIDs, Lars Nielsen
  • What you cite is what you get? Verifiable addressing of immutable, self-describing research data, Eoghan Ó Carragáin
  • Trusted Timestamping via the Bitcoin Blockchain, Bela Gipp
  • Blockchain and the ETH Library – take part, watch and learn, Sven Koesling
