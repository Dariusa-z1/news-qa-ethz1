Reduced services

For the period from Tuesday, 24 December 2019 up to and including Sunday, 5 January 2020, special ETH Zurich opening times and operational hours apply. Buildings may still be accessed with a key or ETH card. All customer desks will be closed at the turn of the year and cannot be reached by telephone or electronic means.

What is the procedure for special incidents or emergencies?

For incidents concerning persons, buildings and infrastructure, the ETH Emergency Desk is at your service over the holiday period.

Emergency Desk

To contact the Emergency Desk, dial 888 for internal calls or +41 44 342 11 88 for external or mobile phone calls.

Maintenance of buildings, facilities and outdoor areas

The Emergency Desk is staffed 24 hours a day throughout the holiday period. Dial +41 44 342 11 88 for external / mobile phone calls or 888 for internal calls.

The ETH card (badge / student identity card) allows access to the student workstations and offices in many of the ETH buildings. You can find more information about this at 24-hour access to student working places. Buildings without an electronic door system may be accessed with a key as usual.

The Hönggerberg underground car parks are accessible throughout the holiday period. In an emergency, please contact the Emergency Desk via +41 44 342 11 88 or 888 (internal calls).

A full winter service will be in operation for all roads on the ETH Zurich premises. However, from 21.12.2019 to 05.01.2020, snow will be cleared only occasionally from the footpaths and there will be no gritting during this period.

MIBAG Property Managers AG (HWW) and Livit FM Services AG (HWA, HWB, HWC) will provide the winter service for the student residences at Hönggerberg.
