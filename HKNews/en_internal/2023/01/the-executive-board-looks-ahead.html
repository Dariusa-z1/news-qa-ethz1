As in previous years, the members of the ETH Zurich Executive Board used this town hall meeting for employees as an opportunity to take stock of the previous year and to look ahead to those topics that are likely to be important in the next.

Joël Mesot, Günther Dissertori, Vanessa Wood, Ulrich Weidmann, Julia Dannath and Robert Perich talked about rising enrolment, energy-saving measures at ETH and the new social and leadership skills, among other topics. Christian Wolfrum also joined the meeting via livestream: the new Vice President for Research introduced himself to the employees at the town hall meeting and gave a preview of how he would like to shape the Vice Presidency going forward.

As the meeting drew to a close, all members of the ETH community were invited to ask questions of the Executive Board members.
