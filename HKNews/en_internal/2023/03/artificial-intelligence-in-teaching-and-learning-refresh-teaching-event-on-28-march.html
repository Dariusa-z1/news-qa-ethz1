Rector Günther Dissertori invites lecturers to discuss the topic of AI in teaching at the Refresh Teaching event on 28 March starting at 12.15 p.m.

An online event with presentations by Professor Mrinmaya Sachan (D-INFK) and Dr Manuel Sudau (D-USYS).

Instead of focusing on how to avoid the interference of AI we invite experts to frame a conversation to help us rethink how and what to teach and test in an era where AI is at everybody’s fingertips and will continue to be so in the future professional lives of our students. The most important question is: What are uniquely human skills and competencies that we need to convey and assess?
