It’s annoying to lose something. Luckily, items found on ETH premises are usually handed in to the Lost and Found run by the Info + Service Centers (ISC).

Now ETH has linked up with the Swiss lost property platform external pageeasyfind.chcall_made – so you can search online for your valuables at any time, even outside opening hours. You simply create a lost property notice, which is then regularly checked against the lost property handed in.

Mass-produced items at ISC

Lost property found on ETH premises can still be handed in or sought directly at ETH Lost and Found i.e. the ISC desk in the corresponding ETH location.
The mass-produced items listed below cannot be recorded online; you should enquire about them at the ISC Lost and Found office:

  • umbrellas
  • hats, gloves, scarves, clothing
  • drink bottles, reusable tableware
  • toys
  • sunglasses (non-corrective)
  • writing material
