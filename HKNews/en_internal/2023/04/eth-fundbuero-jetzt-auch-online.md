## Mass-produced items at ISC

- umbrellas

- hats, gloves, scarves, clothing

- drink bottles, reusable tableware

- toys

- sunglasses (non-corrective)

- writing material

It’s annoying to lose something. Luckily, items found on ETH premises are usually handed in to the Lost and Found run by the Info + Service Centers (ISC) .

Now ETH has linked up with the Swiss lost property platform easyfind.ch – so you can search online for your valuables at any time, even outside opening hours. You simply create a lost property notice, which is then regularly checked against the lost property handed in.
