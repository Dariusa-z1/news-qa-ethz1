- Information on the external pageEuropean Football Championshipcall\_made

The European Football Championship should have actually taken place last year, but the coronavirus pandemic forced it to reschedule to this summer. The games will finally begin on 11 June, kicking off in 11 different cities simultaneously.

You can enhance your European Championship experience by taking part in the betting challenge on ETH’s Point platform. The game is open to all members of the ETH community, who are welcome to participate as individuals or in teams.

## Prizes for winners

The game is already up on Point and ready for you to enter your picks.
