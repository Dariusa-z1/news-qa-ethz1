## How the Coffee Lectures work

• Statista: statistics research made easy! – 2 June (in German)
• What are Persistent Identifiers (PIDs) and why should I use them? – 9 June
• Searching and citing images and icons in presentations – 16 June
• Research data documentation: best practices in file naming and folder structure organisation – 23 June
• What do I have to consider when entering research data into the Research Collection (file formats, preparation of data) – 30 June
Get to know a new tool in our Coffee Lectures that assists you in your academic work or learn important tips and tricks on topics such as persistent identifier, searching and citing images or statistical research.

Each session lasts ten minutes. You will receive concise, condensed information on a particular topic and have time to ask questions at the end. Just the right length of time to enjoy a coffee while you listen.

The online events will be held via Zoom at 3.15 p.m. on Wednesday afternoons from 2 to 30 Juni 2021. Event language is English.

Registration is not required. For browser-based Zoom sessions, we recommend using Chrome.

Good to know : For many of the past Coffee Lectures , a recording and/or the slides of the presentations are available on our website.

## New topics are welcome!

Do you have any questions about a tool or would you like more in-depth information on a topic related to information search, scientific writing and publishing or archiving? Send us your proposed topic to and we will see if it is suitable for a Coffee Lecture.
