Place your bets in ETH’s football challenge

You can enhance your European Championship experience by taking part in the betting challenge on ETH’s Point platform. The game is open to all members of the ETH community, who are welcome to participate as individuals or in teams.

The game is already up on Point and ready for you to enter your picks.
