For many of us, going on holiday means we finally have time to pick up a book again – and thankfully, it’s (almost) as easy to enjoy one on your balcony as on a beach. Have you read a book recently that stuck in your memory? Or is there a classic that you always come back to? Let other ETH members share the enjoyment you got from reading it and send your book recommendation with a brief reason for your choice to by 10 May. Five book recs will be published in the next edition of the ETH employee magazine “life”; the rest will appear in “Internal news”.
