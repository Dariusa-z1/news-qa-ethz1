Are you doing research for a term paper? Do you use current articles from the daily press to create course material? Are you curious and interested and would like to get to know and understand the world from different perspectives, drawing on a variety of viewpoints? Then …

… expand your world knowledge and …

… read and research online: Free access to the electronic version of The New York Times means that you, as a member of ETH Zurich, now have access to all the daily and archived articles of one of the most important American daily newspapers.
Stay on top of your game, set no limits to the knowledge you can acquire and …

… create your user account now, so that …

… you can gain immediate access to the online version of The New York Times. It is quite easy.

  • Go to external pagenytimes.com/passescall_made
  • Click on “Create Account”
  • Enter your ETH Zurich email address
  • Create a password
  • Validate your account via the email you receive
  • You are ready to access The New York Times

Have fun discovering, experiencing and learning.

Good to know: To access The New York Times, it is not necessary to be logged into the ETH Zurich network.
