An event for inspiration and networking

The Swiss Open Cultural Data Hackathon, or GLAMhack for short, takes place once a year. GLAMhack brings together data providers, software developers, digital humanists, artists, Wikipedians and digital enthusiasts online to experiment with unique open cultural data. The focus is on developing and trying out ideas for creative ways to share, use and link open cultural data.

Interactive cultural data

Drawing new knowledge from digital data takes contextualisation and application. That’s why the participants in the Cultural Data Hackathon also investigate concrete ways to use this data and metadata, for example for research purposes, for web and mobile apps, in the context of Wikipedia, for artistic applications or for other forms of reuse.

A multifaceted programme
