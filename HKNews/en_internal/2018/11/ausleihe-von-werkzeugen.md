## Main article

A list of all the devices is available on the Search Portal . The free loan for a maximum of three days is only possible for members of ETH Zurich and, as usual, is performed via the user account using the ETH Card. ETH Library’s normal conditions of use and fees apply. You can collect and return the tools in both Zentrum and Hönggerberg. Further information is available on our website. This service is offered in cooperation with Student Project House and Robert Bosch AG.
