Researchers who register an ORCID iD can guarantee that their publishing output is attributed to the right person.

ETH Zurich would like to use the potential of ORCID, and enables its members to connect their own ORCID iD with their ETH Zurich personal profile, link their research output on the publication platform Research Collection to their own ORCID iD and create publication lists on the internet based on their ORCID iD.

Link your ETH Zurich profile and research output to your ORCID iD today.
