## Quiz on the Annual Report

• In the chapter Services , we present our current service spectrum and the innovative ranges launched in 2017.
• Academic Communication examines the current core work areas and development projects for knowledge provision and academic publishing.
• Information Treasures, Data Curation and Protection of Cultural Property highlights special holdings in our collections and reveals how we prepare them for future science and research.
• Outreach reports on the events organized by ETH Library in 2017.
• The chapter Networking showcases extensive collaborations and offers an insight into the work of the NEBIS network and the Consortium of Swiss University Libraries, which are housed at ETH Library. We also provide information on how we are involved in the establishment and development of the Swiss Library Service Platform (SLSP).
• Library Management is devoted to internal activities for the further development of ETH Library.
• Finally, Facts, Figures, Publications features our performance statistics.
The Annual Report showcases this based on the various projects and activities we tackled in 2017. One of these projects, for instance, was the ETH Zurich’s Research Collection , developed and provided by ETH Library.
