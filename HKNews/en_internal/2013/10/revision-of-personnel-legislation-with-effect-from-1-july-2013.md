# revision-of-personnel-legislation-with-effect-from-1-july-2013

**Source:** en_internal/2013/10/revision-of-personnel-legislation-with-effect-from-1-july-2013.html

**Date processed:** 2025-05-01

## Main article

The Federal Council approved the corresponding amendments to the Personnel Ordinance for the ETH Domain (German, ETH PO) on 14 June 2013. The main changes concern notice periods and the procedure for the termination of employment contracts by the employer. Paternity leave is also being extended from five days to ten days.
