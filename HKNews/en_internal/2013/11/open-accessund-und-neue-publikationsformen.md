## Main article

On 25 November 2013, the Scientific Staff Associations of D-MTEC (ScSt@MTEC) and D-GESS (ASST) together with ETH-Bibliothek would like to invite you to an information and discussion event about Open Access and scholarly publishing.

Monday 25 November 2013, 1:00pm – 2:30pm ETH Zurich, Building IFW, Haldeneggsteig 4, Room C 31

Organisers: ETH-Bibliothek, Associations of Scientific Staff at D-MTEC and D-GESS

Presenter: Barbara Hirschmann, E-Publishing Office, ETH-Bibliothek

Topics: - overview of the latest developments in scientific publishing - possibilities, potentials and challenges of Open Access publishing - Open Access strategies of ETH Zurich and significant research funding organisations - copyright issues that arise when concluding contracts with publishers or when making journal articles freely available on the Internet

After the presentation there will be time for questions and discussions.
