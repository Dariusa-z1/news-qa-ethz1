A variety of construction and renovation projects are planned for the main building over the coming months and years. The Real Estate department will be holding an information session to present future projects for the main building and explain their next steps. The event will take place in the Audi Max auditorium (HG F 30) on 11 January, 2016 from 4 to 4:45 pm.

The session will explore the renovation of the Rämihof forecourt and the MM under the Polyterrasse, as well as the relocation of the Thomas Mann and Max Frisch archives.

Exhibition in the main hall

An exhibition about the renovation of the Rämihof forecourt will also be on display in the main hall of the main building from 14–27 January. The exhibition will feature the winner of the architectural competition for the renovation design, along with other entrants to the competition.
