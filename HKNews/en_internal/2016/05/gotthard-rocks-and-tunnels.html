The Gotthard has inspired people for centuries: tourists, artists, researchers, engineers, workers and geologists have all travelled, explored and drilled through the massif.

And it has also left its mark on ETH-Bibliothek’s holdings: 

  • Files on the construction of the Gotthard Railway
  • Photo reports on the construction of the road tunnel
  • Depictions of the old Gotthard Pass road
  • Rock samples from tunnel bores
  • Geological profiles of the Gotthard Massif
  • Current specialist information on traffic planning, tunnel construction, geology and measuring technology
