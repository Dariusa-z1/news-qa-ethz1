From Monday 11 July, the archive, library and Thomas Mann’s last study will be accessible at the new location with extended opening times: Monday to Friday from 10.00 to 17.00.

The provisional location is on the Hönggerberg Campus:

HCP Building/G Level
Room no. 21.2 – 24.2
Leopold-Ruzicka-Weg 4
8093 Zurich

You can reach Hönggerberg in 15 minutes from Zentrum with the convenient ETH-Link, ETH Zurich’s shuttlebus between the two campuses.  

Once the building has been fully renovated, the University of Zurich will find a new purpose for the Bodmer-Haus, where the Thomas Mann Archive was housed from 1961. The Thomas Mann Archive is scheduled to move to a new, permanent site in ETH Zurich’s main building in the medium term.
