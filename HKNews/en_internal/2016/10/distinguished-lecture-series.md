## Main article

The D-MAVT Distinguished Lecture Series brings world leading scientists and engineers to ETH Zurich to present their work in a colloquium and to meet with its faculty and students. The colloquium has broad attendance by faculty, staff, Master and Doctoral students from all departments of ETH Zurich.

This year’s topics are:

Piezoelectric micromachined ultrasonic transducers in consumer electronics

Prof. David A. Horsley, University of California

17 October 2016 from 5.15 p.m. in HG F30

Flying Carpets: Ultralight Structures for Space Solar Power

Prof. Sergio Pellegrino , California Institute of Technology

14 November 2016 from 5:15 p.m. in HG F30
