## Main article

The orientation map shows the buildings used by ETH Zurich. Several legends help you find a selection of departments, research units, libraries and other ETH facilities, such as canteens and sports facilities. Key public transport connections are also indicated, as are information points where you can obtain assistance with any questions you may have.

The new fold-out plans are available in both German and English.
