# free-open-access-options-for-members-of-eth-zurich

**Source:** en_internal/2016/03/free-open-access-options-for-members-of-eth-zurich.html

## Main article

You can apply for vouchers for the journals of the Royal Society of Chemistry as well as for F1000 Research at the E-Publishing Office of ETH-Bibliothek.

For the journals of the Electrochemical Society , a free "article credit" will be activated, as soon as you choose the open-access option and give your affiliation to ETH Zurich when submitting your manuscript.
