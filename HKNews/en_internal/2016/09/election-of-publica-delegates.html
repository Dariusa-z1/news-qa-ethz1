ETH staff are insured by the federal pension provider Publica through the ETH pension plan. Elections to Publica’s Assembly of Delegates for the four-year term 2017 to 2020 will take place on 27 November 2016. This year, the election will be carried out entirely via e-voting for the first time.

Delegate responsibilities

ETH staff who are interested in this position are invited to run for election. One of the delegates’ key tasks is to elect representatives to the Board of Directors. They are also members of the external pageAssembly of Delegatescall_made, which is made up of 80 employees (16 from the constituency for which ETH Zurich is responsible) and can propose motions to the Board of Directors on all matters relating to Publica.

The external pageBoard of Directorscall_made has 16 members (eight representatives of the employees insured by Publica, and eight representatives of the employers) and is Publica’s highest governing body. It is responsible for the management, oversight and monitoring of Publica’s business administration.

Information about the election                                                                                       

Everyone covered by a Publica plan is able to vote and stand for election. ETH Zurich staff can find key information about the elections (external pageexplanationscall_made and external pagelist of candidatescall_made) on external pagePublica’s websitecall_made (in German). These will provide you with information and documents for your candidacy.
