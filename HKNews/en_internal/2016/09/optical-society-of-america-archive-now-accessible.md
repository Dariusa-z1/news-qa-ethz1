The research papers from the fields of optics and photonics also include historically interesting documents, such as the report on the 1947 society meeting, where physicist Edwin Herbert Land showcased the first polaroid camera as the "most exciting part of the program".

Access is possible from the ETH Zurich network or on ETH-Bibliothek premises.
