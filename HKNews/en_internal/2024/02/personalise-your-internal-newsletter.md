## Compile your own newsletter

- Institutional: News from the Executive Board, information on university strategy or the organisation of ETH, personnel news, information from committees such as the University Assembly or the Staff Committee

- Working at ETH: Everything that concerns the workplace and is important for the individual working day.

- Teaching: Topics relating to teaching and instruction at ETH.

- For researchers: Information for researchers, e.g. library services, the Communication Academy programme, information on knowledge and technology transfer, SSHE courses for researchers.

- Campus: What's currently going on at ETH in addition to work, e.g. regarding buildings, exhibitions, events, catering, offers for ETH members.

- Community: Personal topics, exchange, opinions, discussion formats

- Honours and prizes: Information on which ETH members have recently received an honour or prize.

- Tips and Tricks: Latest articles from the "Tips and Tricks" section for everyday working life at ETH.
