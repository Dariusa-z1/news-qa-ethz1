## Main article

Following intense negotiations with Taylor & Francis, an agreement has been reached by now. The new Read & Publish agreement 2024-​2027 will start retroactively from 1 January 2024 and allows members of ETH Zurich to publish free of charge in all Taylor & Francis gold and hybrid journals as well as the Dove Press and Faculty of 1000 portfolios. The agreement also includes reading access to all content licensed by ETH Zurich as well as extended archive rights. Articles that have been published closed in January 2024, will be published Open Access retroactively.

So far no agreement has yet been reached with Elsevier. Negotiations are continuing and we will inform you about further developments and changes in due course. Further information can be found in the news release of 19 December 2023.
