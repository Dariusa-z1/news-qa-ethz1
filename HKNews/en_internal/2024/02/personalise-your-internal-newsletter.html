ETH’s Internal news has grown steadily over the last few years. More and more units are using the channel to draw attention to news and offers from their sector. As a subscriber to the newsletter, you will now receive an e-mail every two days or so with the latest internal articles.

However, you may not be equally interested in all topics. In order to provide you with the information that is important to you in a more targeted way, we are now introducing thematic sections in Internal news. This allows you to deselect specific topics for which you do not wish to receive a newsletter by e-mail.

Compile your own newsletter

With the customisable e-mail newsletter, you now have the option of receiving regular information on precisely those topics that are of particular interest to you. You can choose from these eight categories:

  • Institutional: News from the Executive Board, information on university strategy or the organisation of ETH, personnel news, information from committees such as the University Assembly or the Staff Committee
  • Working at ETH: Everything that concerns the workplace and is important for the individual working day.
  • Teaching: Topics relating to teaching and instruction at ETH.
  • For researchers: Information for researchers, e.g. library services, the Communication Academy programme, information on knowledge and technology transfer, SSHE courses for researchers.
  • Campus: What's currently going on at ETH in addition to work, e.g. regarding buildings, exhibitions, events, catering, offers for ETH members.
  • Community: Personal topics, exchange, opinions, discussion formats
  • Honours and prizes: Information on which ETH members have recently received an honour or prize.
  • Tips and Tricks: Latest articles from the "Tips and Tricks" section for everyday working life at ETH.

One click for a personalised newsletter

Customising the Internal news newsletter to your personal taste is very easy: at the bottom of the newsletter you receive by e-mail, you will find the link to "Change news alert". This will take you to an overview of the topics you are currently subscribed to and you can customise this selection in the drop-down menu.

By the way: As a current subscriber to the newsletter, you are now automatically registered for all topics. And if you have not yet subscribed to the Internal news newsletter, you can do so here.
