Under the International Programme, Movetia supports cooperation projects between Swiss educational institutions and European and non-European partner institutions to develop joint educational activities. Funding is available as fixed grants between CHF 15,000 and CHF 60,000 CHF, covering a maximum of 60% of the total project costs. The maximum project duration is 24 months

Application deadline is 28 March 2024.

Further information is available on the external pageMovetia websitecall_made.

Erasmus+ cooperation projects

It is also possible to participate in educational cooperation partnerships under the Erasmus+ programme – either as an official partner (third-country status, funded through EU funds) or as an associate partner (funded by Movetia through the external pageSwiss-European Mobility Programme SEMPcall_made).

In contrast to cooperation projects under Erasmus+, projects under the Movetia "International Programme" are led by a Swiss university. The focus is on partnerships worldwide.

For cooperation projects, the application deadlines are 5 March / 1 October 2024 (EU applications) and 19 March / 15 October 2024 (Movetia).
